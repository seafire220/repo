      Characters

        Add a character

        Choose one of these:

        • a .char file made with the character maker on your computer;
        • your own rigged Inochi2D model: its .inp file (as exported from Inochi Creator, which is free), or its .inx;
        • another 2D rigged model (Live2D-style, with a .model3.json file, as used by VTube Studio): a .zip of its folder;
        • a 3D avatar's .vrm file (as made with VRoid Studio; adding one takes up to a minute while its pictures are fitted to this device).

        The device works out which of a model's settings turn its head, blink and open its mouth; the Movement section under the character changes that. Only add models you have the right to use; some model licences say which programs may show them, and a model's own terms are shown with it below.

        Edit

        Describe the character (the persona is written from your answers)

        Or bring the personality of a character from another AI-companion app: choose its character card (a PNG or JSON file). It fills the box below for you to read and change before saving. Use only cards you have the right to use.

        Feelings

        This character has its own expressions. Choose which one shows each feeling; the movement that goes with the feeling plays either way.

        3D animation

        How this 3D avatar moves. Full body plays animations on its whole body: an idle pose for each mood and for listening, thinking and talking, and gestures such as waving, bowing and stretching, with its face, eyes and lip sync on top. Light moves it the way the 2D characters move, and uses a little less of the device.

        Its own animations

        Add VRM Animation files (.vrma, from VRoid Hub, VRM Posing Desktop, or Blender with the VRM add-on). Each can be an idle pose, take the place of a built-in gesture, or be a new gesture it makes when the words call for it. Use only animations you have the right to use.

          Temperament

          How this character moves: calm or lively, reserved or expressive. A lively character moves more and sooner; an expressive one gestures more and bigger; a calm, reserved one keeps still and looks away. It starts from the personality.

          Movement

          Which of the character's own settings each kind of movement uses. The device chooses them by their usual names; change one if a movement looks wrong. An arm that swings the wrong way: tick Reverse. Test moves the character on the device's screen.

          Clone a voice

          Make a custom voice for this character from a clear recording, saved in your own account on the service you choose. Only use a voice you have the right to use.

          Memory of

          The character remembers as a person does: each conversation as a whole, lasting things about you, and a diary in its own words. It all stays on this device.

          Always remember

          Whatever you write here, the character keeps in mind in every conversation (“call me Sam”, “I'm vegetarian”). It never changes this itself.

          What it remembers about you

          Learnt from your conversations, or added by you. Pinned ones are always in mind; the others come up when they matter. A plan with a date is asked about once the day has passed.

          Conversations

          A conversation ends after 20 quiet minutes, a few minutes after a goodbye, when a new day starts, or when you start a new one above. Each is then summed up, and what it learnt is added to its memories.

            Diary

            Each day you talk, the character writes a diary entry in its own voice, at night or the next day.

              AI services

              The device talks through AI services you choose, with your own accounts. Paste each key here: it is stored only on this device, used only to reach that service, and never shown again.

              1. Language model (the character's mind)

              A model running on a computer or server in your home. Nothing you say leaves your home network, and with the device's own voice and speech recognition the character works without the internet at all. The model has to be open to the network: with Ollama, start it with OLLAMA_HOST=0.0.0.0; in LM Studio, turn on Serve on Local Network. For memory that stays right, choose a model of 7 billion parameters or more: smaller ones can chat, but keep poor memories.

                  Device

                  About this device

                  Characters are drawn with Inox2D, the Inochi2D project's player (BSD-2-Clause), or Ayagami (MIT or Apache-2.0). Speech recognition on the device uses Moonshine (MIT) through sherpa-onnx (Apache-2.0). The free on-device voice is a Piper voice trained on the CSTR VCTK Corpus (CC BY 4.0; Yamagishi, Veaux and MacDonald, University of Edinburgh) and NVIDIA's Hi-Fi TTS dataset (CC BY 4.0), with phonemes from espeak-ng (GPL-3.0).

                  Licences and notices: every part of the device's software, its licence and credits, and how to get the source code of its open-source parts.
