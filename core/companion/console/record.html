1. Film the gesture

This is a 3D character, so your whole body is recorded: stand back so the camera sees you from head to feet (a phone propped up works best). If only your top half is in the picture, its legs keep their own movement.

  • Prop the phone up, or ask someone to hold it, so your head and shoulders are in view (and your arms, for a gesture with them).
  • Face the camera, in good light, with nothing bright behind you.
  • Hold still for a moment, make the gesture, then hold still again. Up to 20 seconds.
  • The character moves the way you do, side for side, as if it were you.

Use the live camera instead.
