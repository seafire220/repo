SPEECH, VOICE AND OTHER MODELS ON THIS DEVICE

Speech recognition: Moonshine tiny, English, 8-bit (Useful Sensors Inc.), packaged for sherpa-onnx by the
k2-fsa project. MIT licence; its licence file is below.

The voice: the Piper voice "en_GB-vctk-medium" (the Piper project), MIT licence. It was trained on:
  - the CSTR VCTK Corpus: English Multi-speaker Corpus for CSTR Voice Cloning Toolkit (version 0.92), by
    Junichi Yamagishi, Christophe Veaux and Kirsten MacDonald, The Centre for Speech Technology Research,
    University of Edinburgh, 2019, https://doi.org/10.7488/ds/2645, licensed under the Creative Commons
    Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/);
  - starting from a base voice trained on the Hi-Fi Multi-Speaker English TTS Dataset, by Evelina
    Bakhturina, Vitaly Lavrukhin, Boris Ginsburg and Yang Zhang (NVIDIA, 2021),
    https://www.openslr.org/109/, CC BY 4.0, made from LibriVox public-domain recordings.
  The recordings were changed: they were used to train a speech synthesis model. The voices' names on the
  settings page (accent and region) come from the corpus's speaker information, also CC BY 4.0.
  espeak-ng (GPL-3.0), an operating-system package run as a separate program, turns text into the phonemes
  the voice reads.

Matching memories by meaning: Model2Vec "potion-base-8M" (Minish Lab), distilled from BAAI's
bge-base-en-v1.5. MIT licence.

The wake word: sherpa-onnx's English keyword-spotting model (zipformer, 3.3 million parameters, the k2-fsa
project). Apache License 2.0; its text is among the screen program's licences, and in
/usr/share/common-licenses/Apache-2.0.

Recording a gesture with a phone: MediaPipe for the web (@mediapipe/tasks-vision 1.2.3) and its face
landmarker and pose landmarker (lite and full) models, by Google. Apache License 2.0 (the models' cards say the
same); its text is among the screen program's licences, and in /usr/share/common-licenses/Apache-2.0. The
device gives them, unchanged, to the phone's browser, which runs them on the phone's own video.

sherpa-onnx itself (the k2-fsa project), which runs these models, is under the Apache License 2.0: see the
Python packages.
